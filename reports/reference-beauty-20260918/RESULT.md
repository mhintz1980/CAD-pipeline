# Engine and pump — reference-photo rendering

Completed 18 September 2026. Five 2800 × 2000 PNG images and an editable Blender scene, rendered with Blender 5.1.2 / Cycles OPTIX on an NVIDIA L40S in Modal. Final cloud work took 131 seconds, excluding upload/download.

Open `index.html` to browse the set. All deliverables are in `final-01/`:

- `01-pump-hero.png` — full assembly, pump-facing hero.
- `02-side-hero.png` — full assembly side view.
- `03-drive-side.png` — opposite side, showing the pump and drive connection.
- `04-light-studio.png` — light grey studio treatment.
- `05-pump-detail.png` — close view of the pump and engine connection.
- `engine-pump-reference-look.blend` — materials, studio lighting, four cameras and original CAD meshes. Primary hero camera is active. The alternate light studio is generated by the included script.

Appearance uses the latest three supplied photographs: deep green pump and lifting frame, light grey skid, restrained green engine castings, grey iron, rubber and metallic hardware. These supersede the earlier blue-frame colour study for this render branch. Hex swatches approximate the photographs; they are not calibrated physical paint specifications. No artificial logos or engineering labels were added.
